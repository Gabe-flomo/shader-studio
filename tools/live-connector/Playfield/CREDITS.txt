Playfield Live connector: credits

The way this script talks to Live follows AbletonOSC by Daniel Jones
(ideoforms), https://github.com/ideoforms/AbletonOSC, released under the
MIT licence:

- polling a non-blocking UDP socket on Live's display tick instead of
  using threads,
- recording every listener it adds and removing them all on rebuild and
  on disconnect,
- logging to a file next to the script.

AbletonOSC itself is not bundled; the approach is borrowed with thanks.
